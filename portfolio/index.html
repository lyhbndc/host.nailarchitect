About the Project

Built for a real nail salon, end to end

Nail Architect is a full-stack salon management web app designed for a real home-based nail technician business. The goal was to replace manual booking via chat or DMs with a polished, self-contained platform clients can use directly.

Clients can browse services and the gallery, take a matchmaking quiz to find their ideal nail style, submit a booking with an inspiration image and deposit payment proof, and track their appointment status — all without needing to message the technician directly.

The system also includes a full admin dashboard where the salon owner can manage appointments, view client details, respond to inquiries, export data, and configure automated reminders.

Appointment Booking

Clients fill a form, pick a date & available time slot, upload inspiration images and deposit proof.

Member Accounts with 2FA

Sign-up, email verification, login, and optional two-factor authentication via Google Authenticator.

Admin Dashboard

Manage appointments, view clients, handle inquiries, monitor chat, and export reports as PDF/CSV.

Automated Email Reminders

PHPMailer sends booking confirmations and scheduled appointment reminders to clients automatically.
